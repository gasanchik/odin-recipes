A nice plate of couscous

ingredients

  • one glemulock
  • five teaspoons of effescence
  • one unicorn horn
  • 1kg rice

recipe

  1. chop glemulock up and wait for him to stop screaming
  2. cook at 50 C for ten minutes with oil
  3. pour effescence on his mouth
  4. cook rice
  5. serve and enjoy!
